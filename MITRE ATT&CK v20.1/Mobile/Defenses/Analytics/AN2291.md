# AN2291 - Analytic 2291

[Mobile](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

On managed iOS devices, identify observable delivery of an application package or distribution link through an external website, messaging channel, or other non-approved distribution mechanism.

Where managed network inspection exposes the transaction, identify application-package retrieval or a distribution workflow outside the organization's approved channels. Correlate this with an application newly appearing in device inventory or with a managed-app lifecycle change, using bundle identifier, distribution attributes, and timing where available.

Prioritize cases where the distribution evidence is independently observed and inconsistent with authorized enterprise distribution, alternative marketplaces, or approved testing workflows.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0940#AN2291)

## Mutable elements

- **ApprovedDistributionSources** — Authorized App Store distribution, enterprise application distribution, and permitted alternative distribution mechanisms applicable to the device and region.
- **ApprovedApplicationBundleIDs** — Bundle identifiers of applications authorized for the managed device or organizational role.
- **ApprovedDistributionDomains** — Domains and endpoints permitted to distribute application packages, installation manifests, or other application-delivery artifacts.
- **ApprovedEnterpriseDevelopers** — Authorized enterprise signing identities and development teams, where signing provenance is available to the collection source.
- **ApprovedAlternativeMarketplaces** — Authorized alternative application marketplaces, where supported by the device's OS version and jurisdiction.
- **MonitoredDeliveryChannels** — Email, messaging, web browsing, managed network inspection, and enterprise distribution workflows with available delivery telemetry.
- **DistributionArtifactTypes** — Application packages, installation manifests, marketplace distribution links, and other supported delivery artifacts that the analytic can reliably identify.
- **DeliveryCorrelationWindow** — Maximum interval between observed application-delivery activity and a related application-state change.
- **InventoryObservationInterval** — Expected interval between managed-device application inventory observations, accounting for polling and reporting delays.
- **ManagedApplicationStatusTypes** — Managed-app lifecycle states or status changes used to establish application installation or deployment outcomes.
- **AllowedDistributionExceptions** — Approved enterprise deployments, development builds, TestFlight testing, regional alternative distribution, and other sanctioned workflows.
- **MinimumDeliveryEvidence** — Required network or distribution evidence to establish that an application was delivered through a particular mechanism.
- **MinimumCorrelationConfidence** — Minimum combination of bundle identity, distribution evidence, device identity, and timing required to correlate delivery with an observed application-state change.
