# AN2186 - Analytic 2186

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies Simple Network Management Protocol (SNMP) GET requests targeting system information Object Identifiers (OIDs) from a source not in the approved Network Management System (NMS) Classless Inter-Domain Routing (CIDR) subnet, directed at Industrial Control System (ICS) server or device IPs. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0787#AN2186)
