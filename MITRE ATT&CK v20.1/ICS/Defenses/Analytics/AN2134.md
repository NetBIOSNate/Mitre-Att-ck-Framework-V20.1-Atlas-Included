# AN2134 - Analytic 2134

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies a process on an Industrial Control System (ICS) Linux asset setting a wireless interface to monitor mode via iwconfig or netlink calls, from a User Identifier (UID) not associated with documented wireless diagnostics. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0743#AN2134)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)
