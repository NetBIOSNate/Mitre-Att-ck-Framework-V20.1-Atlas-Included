# AN2202 - Analytic 2202

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies unauthorized Switch Port Analyzer (SPAN) session configuration or port mirroring enabled from a source not in the approved Network Management System (NMS) Classless Inter-Domain Routing (CIDR) on an Industrial Control System (ICS) switch. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.
