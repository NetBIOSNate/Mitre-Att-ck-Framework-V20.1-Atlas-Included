# AN2272 - Analytic 2272

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies a Windows process enabling promiscuous or monitor mode on a wireless adapter on an Industrial Control System (ICS) workstation that is not a designated wireless management station, indicating preparation for wireless interception. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0743#AN2272)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=6 for wireless capture driver on host not in designated wireless management station list · [DC0079 - Driver Load](../Data%20Components/DC0079%20-%20Driver%20Load.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ApprovedWirelessDeviceInventory** — MAC/identity inventory of all devices authorized on the ICS wireless segment.
- **DesignatedWirelessManagementStations** — Hosts authorized to legitimately enable wireless capture for diagnostics.
