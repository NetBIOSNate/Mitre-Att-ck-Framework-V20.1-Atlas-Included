# AN2141 - Analytic 2141

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies an ICS Linux network-facing service spawning a shell, interpreter, or other child process outside the expected service-child baseline. OT operational-fidelity gate: Alert only when execution violates an approved signer/hash/path/parent/process or tool allowlist, or originates from an unauthorized user/tool, and is tied to an OT asset or follow-on OT-relevant action. Suppress vendor-signed software, approved engineering utilities, diagnostics, patching, backup, and documented maintenance.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0767#AN2141)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)

- **auditd:SYSCALL** — Linux Audit SYSCALL/EXECVE/SOCKADDR records (configured audit rule): syscall=execve; shell/interpreter process is spawned by an ICS network-facing service or other unexpected parent process · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
