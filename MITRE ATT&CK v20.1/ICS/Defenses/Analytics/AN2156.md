# AN2156 - Analytic 2156

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies a process opening a raw socket or executing tcpdump/tshark on an Industrial Control System (ICS) Linux asset's network interface, from a User Identifier (UID) that does not normally perform packet capture. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0800#AN2156)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)
