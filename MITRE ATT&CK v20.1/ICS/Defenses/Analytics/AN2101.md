# AN2101 - Analytic 2101

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies a Linux ICS service or process initiating MQTT/AMQP/DDS communications outside its approved broker/client relationship or from an unauthorized runtime/tool. Require communication-matrix violation plus process/destination/protocol corroboration; suppress approved telemetry, historian, gateway, vendor, and maintenance activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0935#AN2101)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)
