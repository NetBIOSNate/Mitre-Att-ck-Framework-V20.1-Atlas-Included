# AN2155 - Analytic 2155

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies Industrial Control System (ICS) Linux service processes initiating outbound application-layer protocol connections to ports/destinations outside the approved communication matrix. OT operational-fidelity gate: Alert only when the asset's documented role does not permit the connection and the destination/proxy/service is outside the approved communications profile, with process/service identity used as corroboration where available. Suppress approved vendor support, update repositories, remote operations, failover paths, and documented temporary connectivity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0799#AN2155)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **auditd:SYSCALL** — Linux Audit SYSCALL/EXECVE/SOCKADDR records (configured audit rule): connect syscall from an ICS service/application process to a destination outside the approved network or vendor-service baseline · [DC0082 - Network Connection Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0082%20-%20Network%20Connection%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ApprovedVendorDomains** — Allowlist of legitimate vendor/business domains for application-layer protocol traffic.
- **NoExternalConnectivityRoleList** — Inventory of ICS hosts whose role does not require external application-layer connectivity.
