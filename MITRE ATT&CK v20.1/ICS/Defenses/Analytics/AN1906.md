# AN1906 - Analytic 1906

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies a single Industrial Control System (ICS) protocol session reading the complete Input/Output (I/O) image from a field device, rather than the sparse, fixed tag subset that legitimate Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) polling queries, from a source not in the approved control server list. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0774#AN1906)

**Platforms**  [Embedded](../../Platforms/Embedded.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **NSM:Content** — single session reads address range covering above full image coverage threshold percent of device total Input/Output (I/O) space, from source not in approved Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) servers · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ApprovedSCADAServers** — IP allowlist of legitimate polling sources.
- **FullImageCoverageThreshold** — Percentage of total device address space read within a single session constituting a full I/O image read.
