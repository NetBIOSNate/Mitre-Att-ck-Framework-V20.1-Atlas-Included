# AN2149 - Analytic 2149

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies an Open Platform Communications (OPC) Unified Architecture (UA) client process on a Linux Industrial Control System (ICS) host calling browse/enumerate operations against the full namespace, from a User Identifier (UID) not associated with recognized monitoring tools. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0788#AN2149)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **NSM:Content** — OPC UA Browse/BrowseNext request to namespace or system-information objects from a client identity or source not in the approved monitoring baseline · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ApprovedOPCClients** — Allowlist of OPC client identities authorized for namespace browsing.
- **ApprovedSCADAServers** — IP allowlist of legitimate polling/browsing sources.
- **NamespaceCoverageThreshold** — Percentage of total device tag/object space accessed within a session constituting full enumeration.
