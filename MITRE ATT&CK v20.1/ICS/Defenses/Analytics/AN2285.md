# AN2285 - Analytic 2285

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies a Windows process joining Industrial Control System (ICS) multicast groups from a process not associated with an approved ICS protection relay monitoring tool. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0909#AN2285)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)
