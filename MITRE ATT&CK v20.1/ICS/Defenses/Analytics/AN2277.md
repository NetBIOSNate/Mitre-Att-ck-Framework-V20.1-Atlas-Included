# AN2277 - Analytic 2277

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies an email client or Office application process spawning a script interpreter or shell process on an Industrial Control System (ICS) engineering or operator workstation, immediately following the opening of an email attachment. Documented across multiple ICS incident reports as a primary initial access vector. OT operational-fidelity gate: Alert only when the account/device/session violates the approved identity + source host/jump path/zone policy and is corroborated by access to a protected OT asset, execution, configuration change, or another security-relevant follow-on event. Suppress approved shift activity, vendor support, break-glass use, commissioning, and documented remote-access windows.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0781#AN2277)
