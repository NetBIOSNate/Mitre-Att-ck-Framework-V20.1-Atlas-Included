# AN2221 - Analytic 2221

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies an embedded field device initiating outbound connections on a commonly-used port to a destination outside the device's documented operational communication profile, which for most embedded Industrial Control System (ICS) devices normally should not include arbitrary outbound internet connectivity under the approved communications architecture. OT operational-fidelity gate: Alert only when the asset's documented role does not permit the connection and the destination/proxy/service is outside the approved communications profile, with process/service identity used as corroboration where available. Suppress approved vendor support, update repositories, remote operations, failover paths, and documented temporary connectivity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0736#AN2221)
