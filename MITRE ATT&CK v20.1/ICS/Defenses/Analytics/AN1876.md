# AN1876 - Analytic 1876

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies indirect evidence of passive RF capture on Industrial Control System (ICS) wireless frequencies -- an unassociated device's RF signature detected without a completed association sequence, or a known device's Media Access Control (MAC) address detected from a location/signal strength inconsistent with its registered installation point. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.
