# AN2210 - Analytic 2210

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies an Open Platform Communications (OPC) client process on an Industrial Control System (ICS) Windows host calling BrowseNamespace or GetProperties to enumerate the full tag tree, from a process not associated with a recognized Human-Machine Interface (HMI) or historian tool. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0788#AN2210)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **opc:applog** — Open Platform Communications (OPC) client not in approved OPC clients calling BrowseNamespace traversing entire tag tree · [DC0038 - Application Log Content](../Data%20Components/DC0038%20-%20Application%20Log%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ApprovedOPCClients** — Allowlist of OPC client identities authorized for namespace browsing.
- **ApprovedSCADAServers** — IP allowlist of legitimate polling/browsing sources.
- **NamespaceCoverageThreshold** — Percentage of total device tag/object space accessed within a session constituting full enumeration.
