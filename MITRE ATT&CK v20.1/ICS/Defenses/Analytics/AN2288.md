# AN2288 - Analytic 2288

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies exploit-pattern ICS/network traffic, including malformed packets, out-of-spec function codes, or overflow-sized payloads, targeting ICS servers or field devices from unexpected sources. OT operational-fidelity gate: Alert only when execution violates an approved signer/hash/path/parent/process or tool allowlist, or originates from an unauthorized user/tool, and is tied to an OT asset or follow-on OT-relevant action. Suppress vendor-signed software, approved engineering utilities, diagnostics, patching, backup, and documented maintenance.
