# AN2284 - Analytic 2284

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies a Windows process sending Industrial Control System (ICS) broadcast discovery frames from a process not associated with a recognized ICS asset management or commissioning tool. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0908#AN2284)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)
