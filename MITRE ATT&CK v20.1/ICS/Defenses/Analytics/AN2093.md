# AN2093 - Analytic 2093

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies node/nodejs or another JavaScript runtime launched from an unexpected service, web process, scheduled task, user, or path, followed by outbound connections, shell execution, persistence, or OT-relevant access. Alert only when the interpreter/shell is not expected for the asset role or user/service context, or when its parent, path, arguments, remote-session source, or follow-on behavior violates the approved OT baseline. Suppress approved engineering scripts, vendor support, diagnostics, backup, patching, and maintenance windows.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0936#AN2093)
