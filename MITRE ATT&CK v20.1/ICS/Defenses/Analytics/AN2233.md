# AN2233 - Analytic 2233

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies broadcast discovery frames on the Industrial Control System (ICS) network from a source Media Access Control (MAC)/Internet Protocol (IP) address not in the approved device inventory. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.
