# AN2109 - Analytic 2109

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Periodic or event-triggered integrity verification identifies field devices with firmware or configuration that differs from the vendor-published authorized baseline, detected by comparing device firmware hashes against the authorized firmware inventory, appearing immediately after a vendor-supplied update or hardware replacement. OT operational-fidelity gate: Alert only when execution violates an approved signer/hash/path/parent/process or tool allowlist, or originates from an unauthorized user/tool, and is tied to an OT asset or follow-on OT-relevant action. Suppress vendor-signed software, approved engineering utilities, diagnostics, patching, backup, and documented maintenance. Monitoring reality: this is an integrity/posture analytic, not a universal streaming field-device event; collection cadence depends on the vendor integrity interface, asset-management system, or acquisition workflow.
