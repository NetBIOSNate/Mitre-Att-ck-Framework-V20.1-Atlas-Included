# AN1932 - Analytic 1932

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies a process loading a packet capture driver or executing a known capture utility on an Industrial Control System (ICS) host's network interface, from a process or account not associated with documented network analysis responsibilities. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0800#AN1932)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode= 6 Npcap/WinPcap driver loaded by image not in authorized capture software · [DC0079 - Driver Load](../Data%20Components/DC0079%20-%20Driver%20Load.md)
- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=1 capture utility execution from account not in approved network analysts · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ApprovedNetworkAnalysts** — Accounts with documented network analysis/diagnostic responsibilities.
- **AuthorizedCaptureSoftware** — Allowlist of approved packet capture tools and processes that legitimately load capture drivers.
