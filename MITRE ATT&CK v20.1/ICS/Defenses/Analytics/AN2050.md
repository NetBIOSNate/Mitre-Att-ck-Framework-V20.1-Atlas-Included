# AN2050 - Analytic 2050

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies execution of port scanning utilities (nmap, masscan) with arguments targeting Industrial Control System (ICS) protocol port ranges on an ICS Windows host. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0907#AN2050)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=1 of port scanning utility with arguments targeting Industrial Control Systems (ICS) protocol port ranges · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)

- **PortScanTimeWindow** — Time window within which connections across multiple ports are considered a single scan event.
