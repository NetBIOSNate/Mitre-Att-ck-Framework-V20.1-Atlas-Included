# AN2234 - Analytic 2234

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies a new Internet Group Management Protocol (IGMP) group join or multicast subscription from a source Media Access Control (MAC)/Internet Protocol (IP) not in the approved device inventory, particularly for protection-relay Generic Object Oriented Substation Event (GOOSE) datasets. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.
