# AN2201 - Analytic 2201

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies a new device on the Industrial Control System (ICS) network placing its Network Interface Card (NIC) in promiscuous mode, detectable by anomalous broadcast traffic handling or unauthorized Switched Port Analyzer (SPAN) session requests on managed switches. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0800#AN2201)
