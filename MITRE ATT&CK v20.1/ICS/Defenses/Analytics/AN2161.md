# AN2161 - Analytic 2161

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies execve of port scanning utilities on an Industrial Control System (ICS) Linux asset with arguments targeting ICS protocol port ranges. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0907#AN2161)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)
