# AN2163 - Analytic 2163

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies a Linux process joining Industrial Control System (ICS) multicast groups via socket() with IP_ADD_MEMBERSHIP, from a User Identifier (UID) not associated with any authorized ICS protection system monitoring application. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0909#AN2163)
