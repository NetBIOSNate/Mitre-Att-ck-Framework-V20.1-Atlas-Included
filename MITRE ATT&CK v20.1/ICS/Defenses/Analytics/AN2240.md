# AN2240 - Analytic 2240

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies malformed, oversized, or out-of-spec ICS protocol messages sent to an embedded field device from an unexpected or unauthorized source. OT operational-fidelity gate: Alert only when execution violates an approved signer/hash/path/parent/process or tool allowlist, or originates from an unauthorized user/tool, and is tied to an OT asset or follow-on OT-relevant action. Suppress vendor-signed software, approved engineering utilities, diagnostics, patching, backup, and documented maintenance.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0767#AN2240)

**Platforms**  [Embedded](../../Platforms/Embedded.md)
