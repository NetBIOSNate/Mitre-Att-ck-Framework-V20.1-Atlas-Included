# AN2220 - Analytic 2220

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies an Industrial Control System (ICS) embedded device executing a sequence of operations inconsistent with its programmed logic, correlating temporally with an operator interaction on an engineering terminal connected to the device. OT operational-fidelity gate: Alert only when the account/device/session violates the approved identity + source host/jump path/zone policy and is corroborated by access to a protected OT asset, execution, configuration change, or another security-relevant follow-on event. Suppress approved shift activity, vendor support, break-glass use, commissioning, and documented remote-access windows.
