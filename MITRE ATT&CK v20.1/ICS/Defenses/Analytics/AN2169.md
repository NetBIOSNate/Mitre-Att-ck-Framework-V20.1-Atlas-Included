# AN2169 - Analytic 2169

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies NetFlow showing a host generating an anomalously high volume of short-duration flows to multiple field device IPs at regular intervals, from a source not in the approved Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) server list. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0727#AN2169)

**Platforms**  [Network Devices](../../Platforms/Network%20Devices.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **netflow:ipfix** — IPFIX/NetFlow flow record (src/dst IP, src/dst port, protocol, packets/bytes/time): source not in approved Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) servers generating high volume of short-duration flows to device subnet at regular intervals · [DC0078 - Network Traffic Flow](../Data%20Components/DC0078%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Flow.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ApprovedOPCClients** — Allowlist of OPC client application identities authorized to connect.
- **ApprovedSCADAServers** — IP allowlist of legitimate SCADA/HMI/historian servers authorized to poll field devices.
- **BaselinePollRate** — Measured normal polling frequency per device, derived from actual baseline traffic capture.
- **KnownTagAddressRanges** — Documented register/tag address ranges that legitimate SCADA polling actually queries.
