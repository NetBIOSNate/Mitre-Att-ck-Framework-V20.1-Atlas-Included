# AN2177 - Analytic 2177

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Real-time monitoring identifies NetFlow showing a source making sequential connections to multiple field device Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, each resulting in large response payloads characteristic of directory listing rather than value reads. OT operational-fidelity gate: Do not alert on novelty, scan/read volume, or enumeration alone. Alert when the source role is not authorized for discovery/polling and the target scope, address/tag range, protocol, or rate deviates from the established operational baseline. Suppress normal SCADA/HMI/historian polling, commissioning, asset inventory, backup, diagnostics, and approved engineering activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0788#AN2177)
