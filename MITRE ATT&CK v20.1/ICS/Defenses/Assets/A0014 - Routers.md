# A0014 - Routers

[ICS](../../README.md) / [Assets](README.md)

A computer that is a gateway between two networks at OSI layer 3 and that relays and directs data packets through that inter-network. The most common form of router operates on IP packets.(Citation: IETF RFC4949 2007)

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/assets/A0014)

**Platforms**  [Embedded](../../Platforms/Embedded.md), [Network Devices](../../Platforms/Network%20Devices.md)
