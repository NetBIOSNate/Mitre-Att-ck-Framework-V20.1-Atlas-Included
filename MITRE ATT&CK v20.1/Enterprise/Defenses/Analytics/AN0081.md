# AN0081 - Analytic 0081

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

User-initiated processes generating sustained outbound traffic over common or non-standard ports, often outside business hours, potentially linked to scanning or proxyjacking. Includes curl, wget, masscan, or proxy clients.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0028#AN0081)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **auditd:SYSCALL** — execve calls with high-frequency or known bandwidth-intensive tools · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **NSM:Flow** — large outbound data flows or long-duration connections · [DC0078 - Network Traffic Flow](../Data%20Components/DC0078%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Flow.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ToolPattern** — Can be tuned for specific bandwidth abuse tools (e.g., proxychains, 3proxy).
- **TrafficRateThreshold** — Baseline deviation thresholds must be environment-specific.
