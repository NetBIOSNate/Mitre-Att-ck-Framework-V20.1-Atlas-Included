# AN0730 - Analytic 0730

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Use unified logs to detect unusual DNS responses correlated with subsequent connections to calculated or non-standard ports. Monitor non-browser apps making repeated outbound connections that deviate from expected patterns.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0262#AN0730)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — DNS responses followed by connections to ports outside standard ranges · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)
- **macos:unifiedlog** — Unexpected processes making network calls based on DNS-derived ports · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ProcessAllowlist** — Expected processes allowed to open non-standard ports (e.g., developer tools)
- **ConnectionVolumeThreshold** — Volume of unusual connections needed before flagging as suspicious
