# AN0330 - Analytic 0330

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Ties inbound access to exposed services (ARD/VNC 5900, SSH 22, ScreenSharing, web services) with process crashes in unified logs and abnormal child processes spawned under those services (e.g., bash, curl) to indicate exploitation.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0118#AN0330)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — process 'crashed'|'EXC_BAD_ACCESS' for sshd, screensharingd, httpd; launchd restarts of these daemons. · [DC0038 - Application Log Content](../Data%20Components/DC0038%20-%20Application%20Log%20Content.md)
- **macos:osquery** — parent_name in ('sshd','httpd','screensharingd') spawning shells or scripting runtimes. · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **NSM:Flow** — Inbound to 22/5900/8080 and follow-on internal connections. · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ServicePortSet** — 22, 5900, 8080/8443 by default.
- **AllowedAdmins** — MDM/jump-host IPs allowed to manage endpoints.
- **TimeWindow** — Default: 10 minutes.
