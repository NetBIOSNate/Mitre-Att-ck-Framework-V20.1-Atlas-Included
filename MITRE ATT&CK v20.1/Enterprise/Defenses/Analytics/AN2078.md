# AN2078 - Analytic 2078

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

An analyst observes an AI agent access $ProtectedDataScope$, invoke a tool/API using that information or a derived payload, and the destination service record a successful transfer to a recipient, repository, tenant, domain, or service outside $AuthorizedAIDataFlows$.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0927#AN2078)

## Mutable elements

- **ProtectedDataScope** — protected information
- **AuthorizedAgentActions** — tools/functions legitimately available for workflow
- **AuthorizedAIDataFlows** — allowed source→tool→destination relationships
- **AuthorizedAIOutputDestinations** — permitted recipients/domains/tenants/repos/connectors
- **AIExfiltrationWindow** — normal access→transfer interval
