# AN0870 - Analytic 0870

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detection of fake or spoofed macOS Security & Privacy GUIs showing healthy status after XProtect, Gatekeeper, or AV processes are disabled. Correlates user-space UI process creation with terminated or missing security daemons.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0311#AN0870)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — Execution of processes mimicking Apple Security & Privacy GUIs · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **macos:unifiedlog** — Termination or disabling of XProtect, Gatekeeper, or third-party AV daemons · [DC0018 - Host Status](../Data%20Components/DC0018%20-%20Host%20Status.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TrustedDaemonList** — Monitored list of macOS security daemons such as XProtect, Gatekeeper, or third-party AV.
