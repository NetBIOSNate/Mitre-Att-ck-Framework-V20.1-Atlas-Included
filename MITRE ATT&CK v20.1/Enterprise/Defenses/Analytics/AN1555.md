# AN1555 - Analytic 1555

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detection of environment variable tampering (HISTFILE, HISTCONTROL, HISTFILESIZE) and absence of expected bash history writes. Correlation of unset or zeroed history variables with active shell sessions is indicative of adversarial evasion.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0563#AN1555)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **auditd:SYSCALL** — execve calls modifying HISTFILE or HISTCONTROL via unset/export · [DC0064 - Command Execution](../Data%20Components/DC0064%20-%20Command%20Execution.md)
- **linux:osquery** — processes modifying environment variables related to history logging · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **MonitoredUsers** — Specific accounts or groups where history logging must always be enforced.
- **TimeWindow** — Correlation period to detect unset/export of history variables during active shells.
