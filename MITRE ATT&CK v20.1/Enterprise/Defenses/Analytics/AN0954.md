# AN0954 - Analytic 0954

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Use of stolen Kerberos tickets or token impersonation resulting in logon sessions from accounts without expected interactive logon events.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0338#AN0954)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WinEventLog:Security** — EventCode=4624, 4648 · [DC0067 - Logon Session Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0067%20-%20Logon%20Session%20Creation.md)
- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=1 · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TimeWindow** — Allows tuning of how far apart related logon and process events can be correlated
- **UserContext** — Customize for high-value or service accounts with restricted access policies
