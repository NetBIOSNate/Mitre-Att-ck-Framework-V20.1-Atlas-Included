# AN1137 - Analytic 1137

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects anomalous usage of local accounts to log into a system, especially accounts not typically used interactively or outside business hours.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0407#AN1137)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WinEventLog:Security** — EventCode=4624, 4648 · [DC0067 - Logon Session Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0067%20-%20Logon%20Session%20Creation.md)
- **WinEventLog:Security** — EventCode=4672 · [DC0088 - Logon Session Metadata](../Data%20Components/DC0088%20-%20Logon%20Session%20Metadata.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TimeWindow** — Tune for normal business hours to reduce false positives from legitimate after-hours work.
- **UserContext** — Define list of legitimate local users for interactive access.
