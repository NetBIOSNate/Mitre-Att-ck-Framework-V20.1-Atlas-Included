# AN1124 - Analytic 1124

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects clients issuing DNS queries with high volume, long subdomain lengths, encoded payload patterns, or to known malicious infrastructure; indicative of DNS-based C2 channels.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0400#AN1124)

**Platforms**  [Network Devices](../../Platforms/Network%20Devices.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **NSM:Flow** — dns.log · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **DomainReputationFeed** — List of suspicious/malicious C2 domains
- **QueryRatePerClient** — Tunable burst rate per IP per second
