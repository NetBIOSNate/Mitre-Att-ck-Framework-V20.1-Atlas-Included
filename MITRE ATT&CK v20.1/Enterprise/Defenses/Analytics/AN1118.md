# AN1118 - Analytic 1118

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Recurring network exfiltration initiated by scheduled or script-based processes exhibiting time-based regularity and consistent external destinations.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0399#AN1118)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=1 · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=3, 22 · [DC0082 - Network Connection Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0082%20-%20Network%20Connection%20Creation.md)
- **WinEventLog:System** — EventCode=106, 200 · [DC0005 - Scheduled Job Metadata](../Data%20Components/DC0005%20-%20Scheduled%20Job%20Metadata.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TimeWindow** — Duration threshold to consider a connection repetitive (e.g., same hour daily)
- **DestIPAllowlist** — Known external destinations to exclude (e.g., approved SFTP/backup servers)
- **ParentProcessBaseline** — Allowlisted job runners or scripts known to schedule legitimate transfers
