# AN1125 - Analytic 1125

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects unusual outbound DNS traffic from ESXi hosts, often from shell scripts, custom daemons, or malicious VIBs interacting with external DNS infrastructure outside the management plane.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0400#AN1125)

**Platforms**  [ESXi](../../Platforms/ESXi.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **esxi:syslog** — /var/log/syslog.log · [DC0078 - Network Traffic Flow](../Data%20Components/DC0078%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Flow.md)
- **NSM:FLow** — dns.log · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **OutboundDNSVolume** — Threshold for data volume and frequency from ESXi IPs
- **KnownGoodVIBs** — Baseline known packages for allowlist comparison
