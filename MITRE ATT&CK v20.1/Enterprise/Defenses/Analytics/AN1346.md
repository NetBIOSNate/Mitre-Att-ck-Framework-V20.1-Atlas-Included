# AN1346 - Analytic 1346

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Behavioral chain: (1) third-party interactive login or mobileconfig-based device enrollment; (2) privilege use or admin group change; (3) lateral movement mounts/ssh. Correlate unified logs and network telemetry.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0488#AN1346)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — loginwindow or sshd successful login events · [DC0067 - Logon Session Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0067%20-%20Logon%20Session%20Creation.md)
- **macos:unifiedlog** — Group membership change for admin or wheel · [DC0088 - Logon Session Metadata](../Data%20Components/DC0088%20-%20Logon%20Session%20Metadata.md)
- **NSM:Flow** — ssh/smb connections to internal resources from third-party devices · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ManagedDeviceList** — Known corp devices; treat unknown devices as higher risk.
