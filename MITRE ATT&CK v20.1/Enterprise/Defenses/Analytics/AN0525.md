# AN0525 - Analytic 0525

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects deletion or hiding of security-related mail rules, audit mailboxes, or calendar/log sync artifacts indicative of tampering post-intrusion.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0184#AN0525)

**Platforms**  [Office Suite](../../Platforms/Office%20Suite.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **m365:exchange** — Remove-InboxRule, Clear-Mailbox · [DC0012 - Scheduled Job Modification](../Data%20Components/DC0012%20-%20Scheduled%20Job%20Modification.md)
- **m365:unified** — PurgeAuditLogs, Remove-MailboxAuditLog · [DC0038 - Application Log Content](../Data%20Components/DC0038%20-%20Application%20Log%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TargetMailboxScope** — Limit by VIP mailboxes or external-facing users
- **AuditLogDepth** — Tune for log deletion following lateral movement
