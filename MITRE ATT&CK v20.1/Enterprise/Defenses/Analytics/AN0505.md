# AN0505 - Analytic 0505

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Spawning of VNC-related processes (e.g., `x11vnc`, `vncserver`) coupled with authentication logs and port listening behavior on TCP 5900.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0178#AN0505)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)
