# AN0151 - Analytic 0151

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Outlook or Word used to forward suspicious internal attachments with macro content. Defender observes attachment forwarding, auto-opening behaviors, or macro prompt interactions.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0054#AN0151)

**Platforms**  [Office Suite](../../Platforms/Office%20Suite.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **m365:unified** — SendOnBehalf, MessageSend, AttachmentPreviewed · [DC0038 - Application Log Content](../Data%20Components/DC0038%20-%20Application%20Log%20Content.md)
- **WinEventLog:Security** — EventCode=4103, 4104, 4105, 4106 · [DC0064 - Command Execution](../Data%20Components/DC0064%20-%20Command%20Execution.md)

## Mutable elements

- **MacroExecutionWindow** — Timing between mail open and macro invocation
- **AttachmentNameHeuristics** — Patterns of known internal spearphishing lures (e.g., invoice, HR_policy)
