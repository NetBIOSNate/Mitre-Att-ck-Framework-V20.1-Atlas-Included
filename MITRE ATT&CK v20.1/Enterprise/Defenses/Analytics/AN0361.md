# AN0361 - Analytic 0361

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Suspicious invocation of GUI utilities or scripts with suppressed or redirected windowing options. Defender view: detection of X11 or Wayland calls to spawn windows that do not appear on active displays, or use of nohup/screen/tmux to mask interactive shells.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0128#AN0361)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **auditd:EXECVE** — Execution of GUI-related binaries with suppressed window/display flags · [DC0064 - Command Execution](../Data%20Components/DC0064%20-%20Command%20Execution.md)
- **auditd:SYSCALL** — Use of fork/exec with DISPLAY unset or redirected · [DC0034 - Process Metadata](../Data%20Components/DC0034%20-%20Process%20Metadata.md)

## Mutable elements

- **DisplayScope** — Restrict monitoring to interactive GUI contexts rather than server/headless processes.
