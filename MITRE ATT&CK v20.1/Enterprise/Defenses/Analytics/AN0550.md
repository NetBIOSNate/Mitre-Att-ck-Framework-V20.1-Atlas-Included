# AN0550 - Analytic 0550

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Abuse of ClickOnce applications where rundll32.exe invokes dfshim.dll with ShOpenVerbApplication or dfsvc.exe spawns unexpected child processes or loads unsigned modules.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0191#AN0550)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WinEventLog:Security** — EventCode=4688 · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=7 · [DC0016 - Module Load](../Data%20Components/DC0016%20-%20Module%20Load.md)
- **WinEventLog:Microsoft-Windows-Security-Mitigations/KernelMode** — ETW telemetry indicating ClickOnce deployment (dfsvc.exe) launching payloads · [DC0034 - Process Metadata](../Data%20Components/DC0034%20-%20Process%20Metadata.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TimeWindow** — The correlation window for dfsvc.exe/rundll32.exe execution and subsequent module loads or child processes (e.g., 0–10 minutes).
- **KnownClickOnceApps** — Whitelist of legitimate ClickOnce applications and paths.
- **SuspiciousChildList** — Child processes considered abnormal when launched by dfsvc.exe or rundll32.exe.
