# AN0033 - Analytic 0033

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Anomalous traffic from ESXi host management daemons (like hostd or vpxa) embedding non-standard payloads in management protocols (e.g., HTTPS) or beaconing behavior.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0011#AN0033)

**Platforms**  [ESXi](../../Platforms/ESXi.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **esxi:vmkernel** — Network activity · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)
- **esxi:hostd** — System service interactions · [DC0082 - Network Connection Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0082%20-%20Network%20Connection%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TLSFingerprintMismatch** — Detects mismatched TLS client behavior vs expected for hostd/vpxa.
- **UnusualDestinationPorts** — Highlight traffic from ESXi hosts to uncommon ports outside vCenter ranges.
