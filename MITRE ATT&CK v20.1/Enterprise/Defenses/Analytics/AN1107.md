# AN1107 - Analytic 1107

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detection of ApplicationImpersonation role assignment or delegated mailbox access to service principals or rarely used users, especially outside of normal hours or geographic norms.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0393#AN1107)

**Platforms**  [Office Suite](../../Platforms/Office%20Suite.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **m365:unified** — Add-MailboxPermission or Set-ManagementRoleAssignment · [DC0038 - Application Log Content](../Data%20Components/DC0038%20-%20Application%20Log%20Content.md)
- **m365:signinlogs** — Unusual sign-in from service principal to user mailbox · [DC0002 - User Account Authentication](../Data%20Components/DC0002%20-%20User%20Account%20Authentication.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TargetMailbox** — Mailbox of interest where impersonation or access delegation occurs.
- **UserAgent** — Tune based on expected application or script-based mailbox access.
- **GeoLocation** — Restrict based on corporate geography or travel expectations.
