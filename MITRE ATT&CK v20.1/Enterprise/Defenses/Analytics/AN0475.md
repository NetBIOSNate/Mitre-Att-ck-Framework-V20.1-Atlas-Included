# AN0475 - Analytic 0475

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Direct write access to /dev/mem or /sys/firmware combined with usage of firmware flashing utilities (e.g., flashrom).

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0167#AN0475)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)

- **auditd:SYSCALL** — execution of known flash tools (e.g., flashrom, fwupd) · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
