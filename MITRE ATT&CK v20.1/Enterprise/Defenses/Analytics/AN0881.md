# AN0881 - Analytic 0881

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Office application auto-loads a non-standard DLL during startup triggered via Office Test Registry key, often without macro warning banners. DLL persistence mechanism circumvents traditional macro defenses.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0315#AN0881)

**Platforms**  [Office Suite](../../Platforms/Office%20Suite.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **m365:unified** — Non-standard Office startup component detected (e.g., unexpected DLL path) · [DC0016 - Module Load](../Data%20Components/DC0016%20-%20Module%20Load.md)
- **m365:office** — Startup execution includes non-default component · [DC0064 - Command Execution](../Data%20Components/DC0064%20-%20Command%20Execution.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TrustedLocationBypass** — DLL may be placed in location trusted by Office configuration or signed to evade alerts
- **AuditPolicyScope** — Only specific tenants or users may have Office auditing enabled at granular DLL load level
