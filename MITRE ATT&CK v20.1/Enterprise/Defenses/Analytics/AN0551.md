# AN0551 - Analytic 0551

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Suspicious creation or modification of inbox rules through PowerShell (New-InboxRule, Set-InboxRule) to automatically delete, move, or hide emails. Defender perspective: unusual rule activity correlated with mailbox access and filtering patterns.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0192#AN0551)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WinEventLog:PowerShell** — EventCode=4103, 4104, 4105, 4106 · [DC0064 - Command Execution](../Data%20Components/DC0064%20-%20Command%20Execution.md)
- **m365:unified** — New-InboxRule or Set-InboxRule events recorded in Exchange Online · [DC0038 - Application Log Content](../Data%20Components/DC0038%20-%20Application%20Log%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **SuspiciousKeywords** — Keywords like 'phish', 'malware', 'suspicious' used in inbox rules to hide emails.
- **UserContext** — Scope mailbox monitoring to high-value users such as executives or admins.
