# AN1189 - Analytic 1189

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects unusual outbound connections to web services from uncommon processes using SSL/TLS, particularly those exhibiting high outbound data volume or persistence.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0425#AN1189)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=3, 22 · [DC0082 - Network Connection Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0082%20-%20Network%20Connection%20Creation.md)
- **NSM:Flow** — SSL/TLS Inspection or PCAP · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ProcessName** — To tune for unexpected or uncommon executables initiating network connections
- **DataTransferThreshold** — Volume of outbound data in short time window (e.g., >1MB in <5 min)
- **TimeWindow** — Look for connections persisting outside of normal business hours
