# AN1218 - Analytic 1218

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects movement of binaries to `~/Library/`, `/System/`, or app bundle locations, especially after initial execution or download from Safari or Mail.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0439#AN1218)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — log stream · [DC0064 - Command Execution](../Data%20Components/DC0064%20-%20Command%20Execution.md)
- **macos:osquery** — file_events · [DC0061 - File Modification](../Data%20Components/DC0061%20-%20File%20Modification.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TargetBundlePathPattern** — Monitor relocation to .app/Contents/MacOS/ or ~/Library/Launch*
- **QuarantineFlagCheck** — Check for disappearance of com.apple.quarantine attribute post-move
