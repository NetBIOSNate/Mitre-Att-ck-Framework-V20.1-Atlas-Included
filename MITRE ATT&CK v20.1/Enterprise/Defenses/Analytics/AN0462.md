# AN0462 - Analytic 0462

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Adversary installs/uses packet-capture or raw-socket capability (WinPcap/Npcap, wpcap/packet DLLs or raw socket attach) and sets a filter. A crafted inbound packet is observed; within a short window the host process that loaded capture libraries initiates an outbound connection (e.g., reverse shell) to the packet origin.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0162#AN0462)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WinEventLog:System** — EventCode=7045 · [DC0060 - Service Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0060%20-%20Service%20Creation.md)
- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=1 · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=3, 22 · [DC0082 - Network Connection Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0082%20-%20Network%20Connection%20Creation.md)
- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=6 · [DC0079 - Driver Load](../Data%20Components/DC0079%20-%20Driver%20Load.md)
- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=7 · [DC0016 - Module Load](../Data%20Components/DC0016%20-%20Module%20Load.md)
- **NSM:Flow** — Single, low-volume inbound packet (REJ/S0/OTH or uncommon dport/protocol) from src_ip followed by outbound SF connection to src_ip. · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TimeWindow** — Seconds to correlate inbound trigger → process library load/driver start → outbound connect (default 120s).
- **CaptureLibIndicators** — DLL/driver names to match (wpcap.dll, packet.dll, npcap.sys, npf.sys) – extend for EDR drivers in your fleet.
- **AllowedInstallers** — Signed/expected processes allowed to install/start Npcap (software distribution tools).
- **ReversePorts** — Likely egress ports to watch after trigger (4444, 53, 80/443, 8080, high ephemeral).
