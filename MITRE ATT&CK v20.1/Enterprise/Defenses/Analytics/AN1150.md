# AN1150 - Analytic 1150

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Monitor unified logs for manipulation of proxy configurations, DNS resolution, or filtering rules. Adversaries may redirect responses or use trusted domains that later resolve to malicious C2 infrastructure.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0411#AN1150)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — System process modifications altering DNS/proxy settings · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **NSM:Flow** — Suspicious changes in TLS certificate responses or redirected domains · [DC0104 - Response Content](../Data%20Components/DC0104%20-%20Response%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TrustedHostingProviders** — Known hosting/CDN providers often abused to hide malicious C2 infrastructure.
