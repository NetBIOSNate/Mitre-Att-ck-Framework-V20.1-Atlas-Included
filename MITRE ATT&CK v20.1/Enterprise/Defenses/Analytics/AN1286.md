# AN1286 - Analytic 1286

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Abuse of system-generated or default privileged accounts such as 'root' or 'vpxuser' logging into ESXi hosts.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0465#AN1286)

**Platforms**  [ESXi](../../Platforms/ESXi.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **esxi:auth** — /var/log/auth.log · [DC0002 - User Account Authentication](../Data%20Components/DC0002%20-%20User%20Account%20Authentication.md)

## Mutable elements

- **AccountName** — If 'vpxuser' is replaced or configured differently, detection logic must reflect the change.
- **IPRange** — Legitimate vCenter IP ranges may be whitelisted to avoid false positives.
