# AN1229 - Analytic 1229

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Suspicious process spawning (e.g., `rundll32`, `svchost`, `powershell`, or `netsh`) followed by network connection creation to internal hosts or uncommon external endpoints on high or non-standard ports.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0445#AN1229)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=3, 22 · [DC0082 - Network Connection Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0082%20-%20Network%20Connection%20Creation.md)
- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=1 · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **NSM:Connections** — Outbound Connection · [DC0078 - Network Traffic Flow](../Data%20Components/DC0078%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Flow.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ParentProcessName** — Legitimate system processes that may rarely spawn network-capable child processes (e.g., `rundll32`, `svchost`).
- **DestinationPort** — Watch for high-numbered ports or well-known proxy ports like 1080, 8080, 4444.
- **TimeWindow** — Capture unusual spikes in outbound connections over a short period.
