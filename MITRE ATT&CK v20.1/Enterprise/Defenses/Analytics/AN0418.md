# AN0418 - Analytic 0418

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Forged SAML tokens can be observed as authentication attempts with valid signatures but missing expected preceding Kerberos or authentication events. Defenders may correlate SAML assertions with absent Event IDs 4769, 1200, or 1202, or tokens issued with abnormal lifetimes, issuers, or claims compared to baseline.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0148#AN0418)

**Platforms**  [Identity Provider](../../Platforms/Identity%20Provider.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **azure:signinlogs** — SAML-based login with anomalous issuer or NotOnOrAfter lifetime · [DC0088 - Logon Session Metadata](../Data%20Components/DC0088%20-%20Logon%20Session%20Metadata.md)
- **WinEventLog:Security** — EventCode=4769, 1200, 1202 · [DC0002 - User Account Authentication](../Data%20Components/DC0002%20-%20User%20Account%20Authentication.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TokenLifetimeThreshold** — Defines the maximum expected lifetime of a SAML token (e.g., >1 hour considered anomalous).
- **TrustedIssuerList** — List of approved SAML issuers and certificate thumbprints.
