# AN1069 - Analytic 1069

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects rogue Wi-Fi access points broadcasting the same SSID as legitimate APs with stronger signal strength, unexpected MAC/BSSID values, or inconsistent encryption settings. Correlates authentication attempts, captive portal redirections, and anomalous traffic flows through unauthorized APs.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0379#AN1069)

**Platforms**  [Network Devices](../../Platforms/Network%20Devices.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WLANLogs:Association** — Multiple APs advertising the same SSID but with different BSSID/MAC or encryption type · [DC0078 - Network Traffic Flow](../Data%20Components/DC0078%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Flow.md)
- **NSM:Flow** — Probe responses from unauthorized APs responding to client probe requests · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)
- **networkdevice:syslog** — Failed authentication requests redirected to non-standard portals · [DC0038 - Application Log Content](../Data%20Components/DC0038%20-%20Application%20Log%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **KnownSSIDs** — Baseline of authorized SSIDs; deviations may indicate rogue AP.
- **AllowedBSSIDs** — Whitelist of BSSID/MAC addresses mapped to corporate SSIDs.
- **SignalStrengthThreshold** — Used to flag unusually strong signals from unexpected APs.
- **CaptivePortalDomains** — Trusted login domains; unrecognized portals may be malicious.
