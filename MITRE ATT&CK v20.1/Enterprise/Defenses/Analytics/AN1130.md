# AN1130 - Analytic 1130

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Discovery of connected SaaS applications, APIs, or configurations within platforms like Salesforce, Slack, or Zoom. Defender perspective includes enumeration of available integrations, abnormal querying of service metadata, and follow-on attempts to exploit or persist via discovered services.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0402#AN1130)

**Platforms**  [SaaS](../../Platforms/SaaS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **saas:adminapi** — ListIntegrations, ListServices: Repeated service discovery requests from accounts without administrative responsibilities · [DC0083 - Cloud Service Enumeration](../Data%20Components/DC0083%20-%20Cloud%20Service%20Enumeration.md)
- **saas:auth** — Login, TokenGranted: Discovery actions tied to anomalous login sessions or tokens · [DC0067 - Logon Session Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0067%20-%20Logon%20Session%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **IntegrationDiscoveryThreshold** — Number of SaaS integrations enumerated before triggering detection.
- **ServiceAccountScope** — Expected permissions for service accounts to distinguish benign from malicious discovery.
