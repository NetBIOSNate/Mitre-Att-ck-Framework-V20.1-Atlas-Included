# AN1419 - Analytic 1419

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects exploitation attempts targeting vulnerable kernel drivers or OS components, often followed by unusual process or token behavior.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0514#AN1419)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=6 · [DC0079 - Driver Load](../Data%20Components/DC0079%20-%20Driver%20Load.md)
- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=1 · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **WinEventLog:Security** — EventCode=4672 · [DC0088 - Logon Session Metadata](../Data%20Components/DC0088%20-%20Logon%20Session%20Metadata.md)

## Mutable elements

- **DriverNamePattern** — Targeted BYOVD drivers may vary based on campaign and tooling.
- **TimeWindow** — Controls temporal linking of driver load → process spawn → privilege use.
- **ParentProcessPath** — Parent-child relationships vary by exploitation vector (e.g., LOLBin vs. dropper).
