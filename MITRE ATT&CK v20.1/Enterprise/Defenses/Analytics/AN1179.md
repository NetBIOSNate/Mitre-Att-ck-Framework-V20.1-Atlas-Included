# AN1179 - Analytic 1179

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Identify processes issuing repeated DNS queries to random-looking domains with abnormal entropy or word concatenations. Correlate resolver logs with high NXDOMAIN rates and auditd socket connections.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0419#AN1179)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **auditd:SYSCALL** — socket/connect · [DC0078 - Network Traffic Flow](../Data%20Components/DC0078%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Flow.md)
- **linux:syslog** — Multiple NXDOMAIN responses and high entropy domains · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **NXDOMAINThreshold** — Ratio of failed queries triggering alert (e.g., >40%)
- **DomainAge** — Flag queries to domains registered in last 7–30 days
