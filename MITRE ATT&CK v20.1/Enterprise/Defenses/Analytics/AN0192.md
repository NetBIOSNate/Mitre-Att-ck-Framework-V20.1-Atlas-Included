# AN0192 - Analytic 0192

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Phishing attempts targeting IdPs often manifest as anomalous login attempts from suspicious email invitations or fake SSO prompts. Detection correlates login flows, MFA bypass attempts, and anomalous geographic patterns following phishing email delivery.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0070#AN0192)

**Platforms**  [Identity Provider](../../Platforms/Identity%20Provider.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **azure:signinlogs** — Failed MFA attempts, unusual conditional access triggers, login attempts from unexpected IP ranges · [DC0067 - Logon Session Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0067%20-%20Logon%20Session%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **GeoAnomalyThreshold** — Allowed distance/time delta between user sign-ins.
- **MFABypassIndicators** — Signals of repeated or anomalous MFA failures linked to phishing campaigns.
