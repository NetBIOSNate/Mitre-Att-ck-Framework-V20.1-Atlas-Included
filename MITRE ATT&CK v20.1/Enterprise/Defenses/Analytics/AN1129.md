# AN1129 - Analytic 1129

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Discovery of SaaS services connected to productivity platforms (e.g., Microsoft 365, Google Workspace). Defender perspective includes unexpected enumeration of enabled services, API integrations, or OAuth applications tied to user accounts.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0402#AN1129)

**Platforms**  [Office Suite](../../Platforms/Office%20Suite.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **m365:unified** — Get-MsolServicePrincipal, ListAppRoles: Service discovery operations executed by accounts not normally performing administrative tasks · [DC0083 - Cloud Service Enumeration](../Data%20Components/DC0083%20-%20Cloud%20Service%20Enumeration.md)
- **m365:signinlogs** — UserLogin: Discovery operations shortly after account logins from new geolocations · [DC0067 - Logon Session Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0067%20-%20Logon%20Session%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **MonitoredAppIntegrations** — Specific Office Suite applications or plugins that may be enumerated or targeted.
- **GeoLocationDeviation** — Geographic deviation threshold for discovery actions linked to recent logins.
