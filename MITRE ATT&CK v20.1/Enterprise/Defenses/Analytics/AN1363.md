# AN1363 - Analytic 1363

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Monitor unified logs for access to payment applications, browser plug-ins, or Apple Pay services from non-standard processes. Detect anomalous use of Automator scripts or keychain extraction targeting financial account credentials.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0495#AN1363)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — Non-standard processes invoking financial applications or payment APIs · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **macos:unifiedlog** — Anomalous keychain access attempts targeting payment credentials · [DC0038 - Application Log Content](../Data%20Components/DC0038%20-%20Application%20Log%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **MonitoredApps** — Financial or payment applications to explicitly monitor for unauthorized use.
