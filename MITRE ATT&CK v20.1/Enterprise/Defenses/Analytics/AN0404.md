# AN0404 - Analytic 0404

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Flows showing encrypted payloads with high entropy not matching TLS handshake patterns, particularly when occurring on non-standard ports. Defender observes NetFlow/IPFIX byte distribution anomalies or IDS/IPS detecting symmetric encryption patterns without associated key exchange.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0143#AN0404)

**Platforms**  [Network Devices](../../Platforms/Network%20Devices.md)

- **NSM:Connections** — Symmetric encryption detected without TLS handshake sequence · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements
