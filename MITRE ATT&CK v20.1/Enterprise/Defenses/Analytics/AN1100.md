# AN1100 - Analytic 1100

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Adversary spawns a process or script to enumerate installed software using WMI, registry, or PowerShell, potentially followed by additional discovery or evasion behavior.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0392#AN1100)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WinEventLog:Security** — EventCode=4688 · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **WinEventLog:PowerShell** — Execution of 'Get-WmiObject Win32_Product' or similar PowerShell cmdlets · [DC0064 - Command Execution](../Data%20Components/DC0064%20-%20Command%20Execution.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TimeWindow** — Detection may be scoped to multiple discovery commands within a short timeframe.
- **ParentProcess** — Tuning based on whether discovery activity stems from suspicious versus approved management tools.
