# AN0668 - Analytic 0668

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects disabling or reconfiguration of syslog or rsyslog services. Monitors sudden stops in logging daemons and suspicious execution of kill or service stop commands targeting syslog processes.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0239#AN0668)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **auditd:SYSCALL** — execve: service stop syslog, systemctl stop rsyslog, kill -9 syslog · [DC0064 - Command Execution](../Data%20Components/DC0064%20-%20Command%20Execution.md)
- **linux:osquery** — unexpected termination of syslog or rsyslog processes · [DC0033 - Process Termination](../Data%20Components/DC0033%20-%20Process%20Termination.md)

## Mutable elements

- **SyslogServiceName** — Service name for syslog daemon, which can differ across distributions.
