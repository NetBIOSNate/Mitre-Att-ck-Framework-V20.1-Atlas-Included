# AN0807 - Analytic 0807

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects direct modification of crontab entries in /var/spool/cron/crontabs/root or /etc/rc.local.d/local.sh followed by execution of scripts linked to lateral movement or malware persistence.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0290#AN0807)

**Platforms**  [ESXi](../../Platforms/ESXi.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **esxi:hostd** — modification of crontab or local.sh entries · [DC0061 - File Modification](../Data%20Components/DC0061%20-%20File%20Modification.md)
- **esxi:cron** — execution of scheduled job · [DC0001 - Scheduled Job Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0001%20-%20Scheduled%20Job%20Creation.md)
- **esxi:vmkernel** — spawned shell or execution environment activity · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **CrontabFileMonitored** — Admins may customize paths in hardened deployments.
- **ShellCommandPayload** — Flag shell-based persistence indicators in local.sh or cron payloads.
- **JobInterval** — Time interval of task repetition for outlier identification.
