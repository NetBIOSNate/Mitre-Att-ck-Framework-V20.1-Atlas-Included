# AN0073 - Analytic 0073

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Abuse of macOS Electron apps by modifying app.asar bundles and spawning child processes (osascript, curl, sh) from Electron executables.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0025#AN0073)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — Electron app spawning unexpected child process · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **macos:osquery** — CREATE/MODIFY: Modification of app.asar inside .app bundle · [DC0039 - File Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0039%20-%20File%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **AllowedAppBundlePaths** — Baseline of legitimate Electron app paths under /Applications.
- **SignedToUnsignedTransition** — Alert when signed Electron parent spawns unsigned child.
