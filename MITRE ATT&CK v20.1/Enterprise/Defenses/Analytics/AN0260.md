# AN0260 - Analytic 0260

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects creation or alteration of LaunchAgents or LaunchDaemons with corresponding plist modification followed by execution of associated binaries.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0094#AN0260)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — process launch · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **fs:fsusage** — disk activity on /Library/LaunchAgents or LaunchDaemons · [DC0039 - File Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0039%20-%20File%20Creation.md)
- **macos:osquery** — launchd_jobs · [DC0001 - Scheduled Job Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0001%20-%20Scheduled%20Job%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **PlistLabel** — Labels not associated with known applications or vendors.
- **LaunchPath** — Executable path outside of standard directories (/usr/bin, /Applications).
- **JobRunInterval** — Unexpected periodic job intervals (e.g., every minute).
