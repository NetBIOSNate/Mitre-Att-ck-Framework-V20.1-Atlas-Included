# AN1014 - Analytic 1014

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Adversary tool/script issuing mass SYN/ACK floods that degrade OS responsiveness and interrupt service response on macOS endpoints.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0356#AN1014)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)
