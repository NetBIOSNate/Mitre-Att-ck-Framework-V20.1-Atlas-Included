# AN0912 - Analytic 0912

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Direct execution of /bin/vmx or presence of rogue .vmx files not registered in vCenter inventory. Defender perspective: anomalous commands in shell history, edits to rc.local.d/local.sh for persistence.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0321#AN0912)

**Platforms**  [ESXi](../../Platforms/ESXi.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **esxi:hostd** — Execution of '/bin/vmx' or modifications to '/etc/rc.local.d/local.sh' · [DC0064 - Command Execution](../Data%20Components/DC0064%20-%20Command%20Execution.md)
- **esxi:vmkernel** — VMX startup messages without associated vCenter inventory records · [DC0028 - Image Metadata](../Data%20Components/DC0028%20-%20Image%20Metadata.md)

## Mutable elements

- **VMInventorySync** — Cross-verify running VMs with vCenter inventory for rogue instances.
