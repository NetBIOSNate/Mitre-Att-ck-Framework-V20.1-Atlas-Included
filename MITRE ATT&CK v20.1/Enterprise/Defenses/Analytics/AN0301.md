# AN0301 - Analytic 0301

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detection of OAuth consent phishing or malicious login attempts initiated through spearphishing links. Behavior chain includes inbound email with OAuth URL → consent page visited → unusual token grants logged in IdP logs.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0107#AN0301)

**Platforms**  [Identity Provider](../../Platforms/Identity%20Provider.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **azure:signinlogs** — ConsentGrant: Suspicious consent grants to non-approved or unknown applications · [DC0038 - Application Log Content](../Data%20Components/DC0038%20-%20Application%20Log%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **AllowedApps** — Whitelisted apps permitted for OAuth consent grants.
- **AnomalousConsentPatterns** — Patterns of consent from unusual geographies, devices, or unapproved applications.
