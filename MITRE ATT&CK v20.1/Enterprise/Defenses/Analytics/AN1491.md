# AN1491 - Analytic 1491

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Persistent or background daemons (e.g., plist or launchd jobs) spawning high-CPU processes like xmrig or cpuminer. Outbound encrypted traffic to IPs/domains commonly used by mining proxies.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0540#AN1491)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — launchd or cron spawning mining binaries · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **macos:unifiedlog** — Persistent outbound connections with consistent periodicity · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **launchd.plist_label** — May be disguised with benign-looking names.
- **DestinationDomain** — Varying mining pool or obfuscated destination.
