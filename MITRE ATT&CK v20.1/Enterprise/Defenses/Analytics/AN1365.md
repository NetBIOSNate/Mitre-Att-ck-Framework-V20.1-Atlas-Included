# AN1365 - Analytic 1365

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Monitor email and document management systems for fraudulent invoices, impersonation of vendors, or BEC-style payment redirections. Detect abnormal editing of invoice templates, or emails containing known fraud language combined with attachment delivery.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0495#AN1365)

**Platforms**  [Office Suite](../../Platforms/Office%20Suite.md)

- **m365:office** — Anomalous editing of invoice or payment document templates · [DC0061 - File Modification](../Data%20Components/DC0061%20-%20File%20Modification.md)
