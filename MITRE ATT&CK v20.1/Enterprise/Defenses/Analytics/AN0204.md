# AN0204 - Analytic 0204

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Anomalous process (e.g., `rundll32`, `svchost`, `cmd`) initiates connections to internal peer hosts not seen in typical communication baselines, used to proxy or forward traffic internally, often using SMB, RPC, or high ports.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0075#AN0204)

**Platforms**  [Windows](../../Platforms/Windows.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=3, 22 · [DC0082 - Network Connection Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0082%20-%20Network%20Connection%20Creation.md)
- **WinEventLog:Sysmon** — EventCode=1 · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **Windows Firewall Log** — SMB over high port · [DC0078 - Network Traffic Flow](../Data%20Components/DC0078%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Flow.md)

## Mutable elements

- **InternalConnectionPattern** — Tune based on known host-to-host communications that are rare (e.g., workstation-to-workstation).
- **DestinationPort** — Focus on unusual internal traffic on ports like 1080, 8080, 4444, or SMB over non-standard ports.
- **TimeWindow** — Correlate unusual traffic bursts with new process execution.
