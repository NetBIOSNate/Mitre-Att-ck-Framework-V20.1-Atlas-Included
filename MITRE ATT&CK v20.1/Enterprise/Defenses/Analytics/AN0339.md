# AN0339 - Analytic 0339

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Deletion or disablement of user accounts in SaaS platforms such as Okta, Salesforce, or Zoom, correlated with anomalous administrative session attributes or multiple account-removal actions within a short duration.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0120#AN0339)

**Platforms**  [SaaS](../../Platforms/SaaS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **saas:okta** — user.lifecycle.deactivate, user.lifecycle.suspend, user.lifecycle.delete.initiated, user.lifecycle.delete.completed · [DC0010 - User Account Modification](../Data%20Components/DC0010%20-%20User%20Account%20Modification.md)

## Mutable elements

- **BulkActionThreshold** — Trigger if multiple account deletion, deactivation, or suspension actions occur within a short period.
- **SessionDeviceType** — Alert when account deletion, deactivation, or suspension is initiated from unfamiliar administrative session contexts.
