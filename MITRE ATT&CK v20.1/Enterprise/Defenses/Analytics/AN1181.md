# AN1181 - Analytic 1181

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Use ESXi syslogs to track abnormal DNS query patterns from management agents or VMs. Identify high-frequency, low-TTL, or unresolvable domains as suspicious. Correlate with unusual management plane process activity.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0419#AN1181)

**Platforms**  [ESXi](../../Platforms/ESXi.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **esxi:syslog** — Frequent DNS queries with high entropy names or NXDOMAIN results · [DC0078 - Network Traffic Flow](../Data%20Components/DC0078%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Flow.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ResolverConfigPaths** — Expected resolver settings for ESXi hosts
- **DomainWhitelist** — Trusted external domains for hypervisor operations
