# AN1479 - Analytic 1479

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects rogue or suspicious wireless access attempts by monitoring firewall, WIDS/WIPS, and controller logs. Focus is on firewall rule changes, rogue AP detection, and anomalous MAC addresses connecting to access points.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0536#AN1479)

**Platforms**  [Network Devices](../../Platforms/Network%20Devices.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **NSM:Firewall** — rule_modification: New or modified firewall rules related to wireless interfaces · [DC0051 - Firewall Rule Modification](../Data%20Components/DC0051%20-%20Firewall%20Rule%20Modification.md)
- **WIDS:AssociationLogs** — Unauthorized AP or anomalous MAC address connection attempts · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **AuthorizedAPList** — Defines known access points and MAC addresses; deviations highlight rogue or unauthorized devices.
