# AN0047 - Analytic 0047

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects unauthorized termination of system daemons or commands issued through launchctl or kill to stop competing services or malware processes. Defenders should monitor unified logs and EDR telemetry for unusual service modifications or terminations.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0015#AN0047)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — launchctl unload, kill, or pkill commands affecting daemons or background services · [DC0064 - Command Execution](../Data%20Components/DC0064%20-%20Command%20Execution.md)
- **macos:osquery** — process_termination: Unexpected termination of processes tied to vulnerable or high-value services · [DC0033 - Process Termination](../Data%20Components/DC0033%20-%20Process%20Termination.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ProtectedServiceList** — Defines macOS services (e.g., securityd, keychain-related daemons) that should never be disabled.
