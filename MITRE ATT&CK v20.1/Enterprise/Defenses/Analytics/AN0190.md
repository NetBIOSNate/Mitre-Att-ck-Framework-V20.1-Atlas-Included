# AN0190 - Analytic 0190

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detection of phishing through anomalous Mail app activity, such as attachments saved to disk and immediately executed, or Safari/Preview launching URLs and files linked from email messages. Correlate UnifiedLogs events with subsequent process execution.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0070#AN0190)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — Inbound email activity with suspicious domains or mismatched sender information · [DC0038 - Application Log Content](../Data%20Components/DC0038%20-%20Application%20Log%20Content.md)
- **macos:unifiedlog** — Preview.app, Safari.app, or Mail.app spawning new processes outside normal patterns · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **SuspiciousDomains** — List of domains known for phishing activity or suspicious sender infrastructure.
- **ExecutionDelayWindow** — Time threshold between file save and execution considered suspicious.
