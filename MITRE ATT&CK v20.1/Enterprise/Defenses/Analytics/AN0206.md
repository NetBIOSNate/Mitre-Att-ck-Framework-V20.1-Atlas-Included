# AN0206 - Analytic 0206

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Execution of AppleScript or Automator services launching `ssh -L`, `socat`, or `launchctl` items that dynamically reroute traffic from one Mac endpoint to another. LaunchAgents used to establish permanent internal tunnels.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0075#AN0206)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — None · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **NSM:Flow** — pf firewall logs · [DC0078 - Network Traffic Flow](../Data%20Components/DC0078%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Flow.md)
- **macos:osquery** — Process Events and Launch Daemons · [DC0060 - Service Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0060%20-%20Service%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **LaunchAgentPath** — Directory where proxying LaunchDaemons may be dropped, e.g., `/Library/LaunchDaemons/`.
- **PortBindings** — Dynamic port forwards often use ephemeral or non-standard service ports.
- **AppleScriptUsage** — May trigger on less common scripting interfaces for traffic redirection.
