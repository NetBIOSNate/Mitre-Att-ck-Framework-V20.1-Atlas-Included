# AN1364 - Analytic 1364

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Monitor SaaS financial systems (e.g., QuickBooks, Workday, SAP S/4HANA cloud) for unauthorized access, rule changes, or mass export of financial data. Detect anomalous transfers initiated via SaaS APIs or new MFA-disabled logins targeting finance apps.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0495#AN1364)

**Platforms**  [SaaS](../../Platforms/SaaS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **saas:finance** — Transaction/Transfer: Unusual or large transactions initiated outside business hours or by unusual accounts · [DC0038 - Application Log Content](../Data%20Components/DC0038%20-%20Application%20Log%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TransactionThreshold** — Customizable monetary threshold above which financial transactions should be flagged.
