# AN0415 - Analytic 0415

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Adversary destroys virtual disks (VMDK), images, or VMs by invoking `vim-cmd`, deleting datastore contents, or purging snapshots.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0146#AN0415)

**Platforms**  [ESXi](../../Platforms/ESXi.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **esxi:vmkernel** — file delete|datastore purge · [DC0098 - Volume Deletion](../Data%20Components/DC0098%20-%20Volume%20Deletion.md)

## Mutable elements

- **DatastorePath** — Targeted deletion of critical VMDKs or VM configuration files.
- **InitiatingUser** — Detect deletions from users outside normal maintenance windows.
