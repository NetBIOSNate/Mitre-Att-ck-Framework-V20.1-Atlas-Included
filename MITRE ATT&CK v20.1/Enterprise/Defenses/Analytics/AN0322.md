# AN0322 - Analytic 0322

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Phishing attempts via iCloud Mail, Gmail, or social media apps accessed on macOS systems. Defender view includes Mail.app or Safari downloads of files followed by osascript, Terminal, or abnormal child process execution.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0115#AN0322)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — Received messages containing embedded links or attachments from non-enterprise services · [DC0038 - Application Log Content](../Data%20Components/DC0038%20-%20Application%20Log%20Content.md)
- **macos:unifiedlog** — Execution of osascript, bash, or Terminal initiated from Mail.app or Safari · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **macos:unifiedlog** — Suspicious outbound HTTPS requests to domains flagged as newly registered or untrusted after spearphishing message interaction · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)

## Mutable elements

- **CertificateChecks** — Flagging mismatched or self-signed certificates during outbound connections initiated after spearphishing messages.
- **ExecutionDelay** — Window of time between attachment download and subsequent suspicious execution.
