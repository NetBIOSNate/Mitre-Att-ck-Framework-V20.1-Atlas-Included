# AN1445 - Analytic 1445

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects attempts to forge or replay Kerberos tickets by monitoring Unified Logs for anomalous kinit/klist activity and correlating unusual authentication sequences.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0522#AN1445)

**Platforms**  [macOS](../../Platforms/macOS.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **macos:unifiedlog** — Unusual Kerberos TGS-REQ without TGT or anomalous ticket lifetime · [DC0088 - Logon Session Metadata](../Data%20Components/DC0088%20-%20Logon%20Session%20Metadata.md)

## Mutable elements

- **TicketRequestPatterns** — Expected sequence of TGT followed by TGS requests; deviations may indicate forgery.
- **TicketLifetime** — Expected ticket lifetimes; anomalies may indicate Golden or Silver Tickets.
