# AN0939 - Analytic 0939

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detection of maintainer scripts (e.g., postinst, preinst) being modified or executed during dpkg or rpm operations. Watch for script content that spawns additional processes or writes outside package scope.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0330#AN0939)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **auditd:SYSCALL** — Execution of dpkg or rpm followed by fork/execve from within postinst, prerm, etc. · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **auditd:SYSCALL** — write · [DC0039 - File Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0039%20-%20File%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **ScriptName** — May be postinst, preinst, prerm, or postrm depending on packaging system
- **PackageManager** — Depends on system: dpkg, apt, rpm, yum, etc.
