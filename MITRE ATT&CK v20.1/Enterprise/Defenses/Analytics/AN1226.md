# AN1226 - Analytic 1226

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Detects suspicious curl, wget, or custom socket traffic that leverages DNS, HTTPS, or IRC-style protocols with unbalanced traffic or beacon-like intervals.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0444#AN1226)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **NSM:Flow** — dns, ssl, conn · [DC0085 - Network Traffic Content](../Data%20Components/DC0085%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Content.md)
- **auditd:SYSCALL** — execve · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)

## Mutable elements

- **KnownPortsToMonitor** — Uncommon ports for HTTPS, IRC, DNS (e.g., 8443, 5353)
- **BeaconTimingThreshold** — Detect intervals of outbound traffic within fixed timeframes
