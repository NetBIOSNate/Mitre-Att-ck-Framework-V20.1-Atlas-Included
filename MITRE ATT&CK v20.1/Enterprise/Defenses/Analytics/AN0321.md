# AN0321 - Analytic 0321

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Analytics](README.md)

Use of non-enterprise email or messaging services in Thunderbird, Evolution, or browsers leading to suspicious file downloads and subsequent execution. Defender view includes browser-initiated downloads of unexpected content and shell or interpreter processes launched post-download.

[MITRE ATT&CK record](https://attack.mitre.org/detectionstrategies/DET0115#AN0321)

**Platforms**  [Linux](../../Platforms/Linux.md)

## Log sources and channels

- **auditd:SYSCALL** — execve: Execution of bash, python, or perl processes spawned by browser/email client · [DC0032 - Process Creation](../Data%20Components/DC0032%20-%20Process%20Creation.md)
- **linux:syslog** — Inbound messages from webmail services containing attachments or URLs · [DC0038 - Application Log Content](../Data%20Components/DC0038%20-%20Application%20Log%20Content.md)
- **NSM:Flow** — Outbound traffic to domains/IPs not previously resolved, occurring shortly after attachment download or link click · [DC0078 - Network Traffic Flow](../Data%20Components/DC0078%20-%20Network%20Traffic%20Flow.md)

## Mutable elements

- **BrowserProcesses** — Configured list of browsers or email clients to monitor (e.g., firefox, chromium, thunderbird).
- **PhishingIndicators** — Custom regex rules for suspicious URL patterns, file extensions, or encoded links.
