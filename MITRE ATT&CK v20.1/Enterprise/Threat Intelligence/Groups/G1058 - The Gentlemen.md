# G1058 - The Gentlemen

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Groups](README.md)

[The Gentlemen](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1058) is a group that maintains the [The Gentlemen Ransomware](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S9051)-as-a-service (RaaS). [The Gentlemen](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1058) was first observed in mid-2025 who had posed [The Gentlemen Ransomware](https://attack.mitre.org/software/S9051) as a closed ransomware and shortly later opened the ransomware as a RaaS to affiliates in September 2025. [The Gentlemen](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G1058) has been affiliated with the threat group Storm-2697 and GOLD SHERWOOD.(Citation: MSTI_GentelmenRansomware_May2026)(Citation: Intel471_TheGentlemen_Aug2026)(Citation: Sophos_TheGentlemen_Sept2026)(Citation: SoučekESET_Gentlemen_Jun2026)(Citation: CheckPointResearch_TheGentlemen_April2026)(Citation: BradyUnit42_TheGentlemen_July2026)(Citation: CarveyODonnellWelchHuntress_TheGentlemen_May2026)(Citation: TrendAI_TheGentlemen_Sept2025)
