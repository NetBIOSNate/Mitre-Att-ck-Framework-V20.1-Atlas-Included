# G0039 - Suckfly

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Groups](README.md)

[Suckfly](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0039) is a China-based threat group that has been active since at least 2014. (Citation: Symantec Suckfly March 2016)
