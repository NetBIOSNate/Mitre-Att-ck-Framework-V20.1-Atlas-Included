# G0120 - Evilnum

[Enterprise](../../README.md) / [Groups](README.md)

[Evilnum](https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0120) is a financially motivated threat group that has been active since at least 2018.(Citation: ESET EvilNum July 2020)
