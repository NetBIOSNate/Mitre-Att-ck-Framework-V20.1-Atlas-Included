# AML.T0136 - Exfiltration via AI User Interface

[MITRE ATLAS](../../../README.md) / [AML.TA0010 - Exfiltration](../README.md)

Adversaries exfiltrate information through an AI system's user-facing outputs. Information may be disclosed in conversational responses, generated documents, previews, or other content presented through the application. The application may expose information available to a victim-controlled AI service or deliver information gathered from other victim systems.

Adversaries may direct an AI system to present sensitive information in responses or as downloadable artifacts. The application's user interface serves as the transfer channel through which adversaries receive the information.

Transferred information may include [Data from AI Services](../../13%20AML.TA0009%20-%20Collection/AML.T0085%20-%20Data%20from%20AI%20Services/README.md) or [Outputs from AI Models](../../13%20AML.TA0009%20-%20Collection/AML.T0135%20-%20Outputs%20from%20AI%20Models/README.md).
