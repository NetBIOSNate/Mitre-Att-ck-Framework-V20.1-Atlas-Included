# AML.T0017.000 - Adversarial AI Attacks

[MITRE ATLAS](../../../../README.md) / [AML.TA0003 - Resource Development](../../README.md) / [AML.T0017 - Develop Capabilities](../README.md)

Adversaries may develop their own adversarial attacks.
They may leverage existing libraries as a starting point ([Adversarial AI Attack Implementations](../../AML.T0016%20-%20Obtain%20Capabilities/AML.T0016.000%20-%20Adversarial%20AI%20Attack%20Implementations/README.md)).
They may implement ideas described in public research papers or develop custom made attacks for the victim model.

**Tactics**  [AML.TA0003 - Resource Development](../../README.md)

**Platforms**  [Predictive AI](../../../../Platforms/Predictive%20AI.md), [Generative AI](../../../../Platforms/Generative%20AI.md), [Agentic AI](../../../../Platforms/Agentic%20AI.md)

**Maturity**  Demonstrated
