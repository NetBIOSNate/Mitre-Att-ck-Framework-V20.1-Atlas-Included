# AML.T0047 - AI-Enabled Product or Service

[MITRE ATLAS](../../../README.md) / [AML.TA0000 - AI Model Access](../README.md)

Adversaries may use a product or service that uses artificial intelligence under the hood to gain access to the underlying AI model.
This type of indirect model access may reveal details of the AI model or its inferences in logs or metadata.

**Tactics**  [AML.TA0000 - AI Model Access](../README.md)

**Platforms**  [Predictive AI](../../../Platforms/Predictive%20AI.md), [Generative AI](../../../Platforms/Generative%20AI.md), [Agentic AI](../../../Platforms/Agentic%20AI.md)

**Maturity**  Realized
