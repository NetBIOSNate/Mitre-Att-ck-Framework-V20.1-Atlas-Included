# AML.E0010 - Zenity

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Zenity](AML.E0010%20-%20Zenity.md) develops security and governance technology for enterprise AI agents, copilots, and low-code or no-code applications. Their work addresses the risks introduced as business users build applications and connect agents to organizational data and services. [group-profile](https://zenity.io/company) Their research team, Zenity Labs, publishes technical investigations of vulnerabilities in deployed AI agents and enterprise applications, including indirect prompt injection, agent hijacking, and zero-click data exfiltration. Their publications explain attack paths and provide defensive recommendations. [research-profile](https://labs.zenity.io/)
