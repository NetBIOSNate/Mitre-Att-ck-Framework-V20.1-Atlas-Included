# AML.E0029 - Cybernews Investigation Team

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Cybernews Investigation Team](AML.E0029%20-%20Cybernews%20Investigation%20Team.md) is the security research team at Cybernews, a technology and cybersecurity publication. They use white-hat hacking techniques to identify and responsibly disclose cybersecurity threats and vulnerabilities. Their investigations cover personal data leaks, enterprise security weaknesses, and exchanges of sensitive information on the dark web. [group-profile](https://cybernews.com/about-us/)
