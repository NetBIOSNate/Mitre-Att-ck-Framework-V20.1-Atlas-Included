# AML.E0027 - Storm-2139

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Storm-2139](AML.E0027%20-%20Storm-2139.md) is a global cybercrime group who has monetized unauthorized access to generative-AI services. Members of the group have exploited exposed customer credentials scraped from public sources, accessed AI-service accounts, and developed tools to bypass safeguards. They resold access and supplied instructions for generating prohibited synthetic content, including non-consensual intimate images. [group-profile](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2025/02/27/disrupting-cybercrime-abusing-gen-ai/)

The group includes creators who develop tools, providers who modify and distribute those tools through different service and payment tiers, and users who generate content. Identified participants operated in multiple countries, including Iran, the United Kingdom, China, Vietnam, and the United States. [group-profile](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2025/02/27/disrupting-cybercrime-abusing-gen-ai/)
