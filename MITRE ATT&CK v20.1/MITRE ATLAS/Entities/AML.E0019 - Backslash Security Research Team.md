# AML.E0019 - Backslash Security Research Team

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Backslash Security Research Team](AML.E0019%20-%20Backslash%20Security%20Research%20Team.md) investigates the security of AI coding assistants, autonomous agents, MCP servers, skills, and plugins used on enterprise endpoints. They conduct vulnerability research, adversarial testing of agentic AI tools, and analysis of the open-source components supporting those tools. They also publish free assessment tools and work with vendors on coordinated vulnerability disclosure. [team-profile](https://www.backslash.security/blog/hundreds-of-mcp-servers-vulnerable-to-abuse) They are part of Backslash Security, a company developing security controls for enterprise use of agentic AI. [group-profile](https://www.backslash.security/about)
