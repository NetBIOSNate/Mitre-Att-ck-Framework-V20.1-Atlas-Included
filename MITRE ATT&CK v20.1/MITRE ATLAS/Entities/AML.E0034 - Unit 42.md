# AML.E0034 - Unit 42

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Unit 42](AML.E0034%20-%20Unit%2042.md) is the threat intelligence, incident response, and security consulting team at Palo Alto Networks. They combine threat research, investigative expertise, and global telemetry to help organizations prepare for, respond to, and recover from cyber attacks. Their work includes collecting and analyzing threat intelligence, investigating incidents, assessing security readiness, and improving security controls and operations.
