# AML.E0030 - Check Point Research

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Check Point Research](AML.E0030%20-%20Check%20Point%20Research.md) is the threat intelligence and security research team at Check Point Software Technologies. They analyze global cyber attack telemetry from the ThreatCloud network. They publish research findings from malware analysis, machine learning, anomaly detection, reverse engineering, and campaign hunting activities. They collaborate with other security vendors, law enforcement, and CERTs. [group-profile](https://research.checkpoint.com/about-us/)
