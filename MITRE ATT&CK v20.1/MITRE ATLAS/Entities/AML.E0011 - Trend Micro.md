# AML.E0011 - Trend Micro

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Trend Micro](AML.E0011%20-%20Trend%20Micro.md) is a cybersecurity company providing security products and services for enterprise environments. Their researchers investigate threats, vulnerabilities, and risks affecting cloud infrastructure, containers, and AI systems. [group-profile](https://www.trendmicro.com/en_us/about.html) Their published research includes analysis of exposed container registries and how unauthorized access can enable tampering with AI models. [team-research](https://www.trendmicro.com/vinfo/br/security/news/cyber-attacks/silent-sabotage-weaponizing-ai-models-in-exposed-containers)
