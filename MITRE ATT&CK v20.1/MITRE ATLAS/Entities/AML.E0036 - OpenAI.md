# AML.E0036 - OpenAI

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[OpenAI](AML.E0036%20-%20OpenAI.md) develops frontier AI models and deploys them through products including ChatGPT, Codex, and a developer API platform. Their research advances model capabilities while evaluating safety risks and developing safeguards for deployment. They publish model evaluations and system cards documenting capabilities, limitations, and safety findings. [group-profile](https://openai.com/about/)
