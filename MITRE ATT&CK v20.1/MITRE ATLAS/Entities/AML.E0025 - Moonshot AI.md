# AML.E0025 - Moonshot AI

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Moonshot AI](AML.E0025%20-%20Moonshot%20AI.md) is a Chinese AI company that developes the Kimi family of AI models and applications. Their stated research objective is to advance artificial general intelligence while sharing research with the open source community. Their models and products support coding, analysis, reasoning, and knowledge-work tasks, with access through user-facing applications and developer APIs. [group-profile](https://www.moonshot.ai/)
