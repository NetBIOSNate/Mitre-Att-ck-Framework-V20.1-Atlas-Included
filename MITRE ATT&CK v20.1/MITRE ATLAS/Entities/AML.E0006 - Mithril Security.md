# AML.E0006 - Mithril Security

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Mithril Security](AML.E0006%20-%20Mithril%20Security.md) develops infrastructure for confidential and verifiable AI. Their work focuses on model provenance, protection of model intellectual property, and confidentiality of data processed by AI systems. Their tools include BlindLlama for confidential AI inference and AICert, which uses hardware-backed attestation to link AI models to their training procedures and datasets. [group-profile](https://mithrilsecurity.io/)

**Entity type**  Organization
