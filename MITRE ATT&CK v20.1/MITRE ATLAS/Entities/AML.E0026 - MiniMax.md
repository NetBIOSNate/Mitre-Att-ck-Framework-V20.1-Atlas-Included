# AML.E0026 - MiniMax

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[MiniMax](AML.E0026%20-%20MiniMax.md) is a Chinese AI technology company developing multimodal foundation models. They develop models that process and generate text, audio, images, video, and music. They provide AI applications and a APIs to enterprises and developers, with capabilities including coding, agentic tasks, and long-context processing. [group-profile](https://www.minimax.io/about)

**Entity type**  Organization
