# AML.E0031 - Microsoft Defender Security Research Team

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Microsoft Defender Security Research Team](AML.E0031%20-%20Microsoft%20Defender%20Security%20Research%20Team.md) investigates threats affecting Microsoft customers and the wider security ecosystem. Their published research covers malware, phishing, identity abuse, software supply chain attacks, and security weaknesses in AI applications and agents. They analyze attack techniques and provide guidance for detection, mitigation, and defense. [group-profile](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/author/windows-defender-research/)
