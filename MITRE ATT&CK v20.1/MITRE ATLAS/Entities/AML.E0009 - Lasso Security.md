# AML.E0009 - Lasso Security

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Lasso Security](AML.E0009%20-%20Lasso%20Security.md) develops security controls for enterprise use of generative AI. Their platform discovers and monitors AI interactions and supports detection and response to risks affecting models, agents, applications, and sensitive data. Their team brings together cybersecurity and AI practitioners. [group-profile](https://www.lasso.security/the-team)
