# AML.E0012 - iProov Red Team

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[iProov Red Team](AML.E0012%20-%20iProov%20Red%20Team.md) evaluates the security of iProov systems by identifying vulnerabilities and researching improvements to their defenses. Their work includes attacking biometric verification systems and strengthening the protocols and algorithms used to resist evolving threats such as deepfakes. [team-profile](https://www.iproov.com/blog/meet-the-team-panos) They operate within iProov, a provider of biometric identity verification and liveness detection. [group-profile](https://www.iproov.com/)

**Entity type**  Team

## Uses

- [AML.S0011 - OBS Studio](../Software/Tools/AML.S0011%20-%20OBS%20Studio.md) — The [iProov Red Team](AML.E0012%20-%20iProov%20Red%20Team.md) used [OBS Studio](../Software/Tools/AML.S0011%20-%20OBS%20Studio.md) to stream manipulated video for presentation to a mobile biometric verification system.
- [AML.S0013 - Swapface](../Software/Tools/AML.S0013%20-%20Swapface.md) — The [iProov Red Team](AML.E0012%20-%20iProov%20Red%20Team.md) used [Swapface](../Software/Tools/AML.S0013%20-%20Swapface.md) to create a live face-swapped video feed.
- [AML.S0014 - Virtual Camera: Live Assist](../Software/Tools/AML.S0014%20-%20Virtual%20Camera%20-%20Live%20Assist.md) — The [iProov Red Team](AML.E0012%20-%20iProov%20Red%20Team.md) used [Virtual Camera: Live Assist](../Software/Tools/AML.S0014%20-%20Virtual%20Camera%20-%20Live%20Assist.md) to replace the camera feed presented to a mobile facial-verification system with a manipulated video stream.
