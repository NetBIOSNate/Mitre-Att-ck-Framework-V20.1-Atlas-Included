# AML.E0014 - PromptArmor

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[PromptArmor](AML.E0014%20-%20PromptArmor.md) provides AI risk intelligence for organizations assessing and monitoring AI-enabled vendors. Their work examines application construction, connector scope, data access, model-provider practices, and emerging threats. Their services support enterprise AI governance and third-party risk management. [group-profile](https://www.promptarmor.com/)
