# AML.E0032 - SafeBreach Labs

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[SafeBreach Labs](AML.E0032%20-%20SafeBreach%20Labs.md) is the security research team at SafeBreach. They conduct threat intelligence and vulnerability research, and monitor underground activity and intelligence feeds. Their findings inform SafeBreach's "Hacker's Playbook," enabling organizations to test their defenses against emerging threats. They also publish original security research and share findings with the broader community. [group-profile](https://www.safebreach.com/safebreach-labs/)
