# AML.E0039 - Anthropic

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Anthropic](AML.E0039%20-%20Anthropic.md) develops frontier AI models and deploys them through products and partnerships, including Claude. Their research covers model capabilities, interpretability, alignment, and safety techniques, alongside the economic and societal impacts of AI. They apply research findings to deployed systems, publish their findings, and collaborate with government, academia, industry, and civil society on AI safety. [group-profile](https://www.anthropic.com/company)
