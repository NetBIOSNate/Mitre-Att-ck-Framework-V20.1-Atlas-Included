# AML.E0000 - MITRE AI Red Team

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

The [MITRE AI Red Team](AML.E0000%20-%20MITRE%20AI%20Red%20Team.md) supports the U.S. government by evaluating AI-enabled systems to identify vulnerabilities and improve their security and resilience. The team authored playbook for AI red teams, which describes a repeatable red-teaming process with collaboration among system developers, security teams, and other stakeholders within a Build-Attack-Defend framework. [red-team-playbook](https://www.spiedigitallibrary.org/conference-proceedings-of-spie/13054/130540B/An-AI-red-team-playbook/10.1117/12.3021906.short) MITRE is a nonprofit organization that operates federally funded research and development centers, providing independent technical expertise and systems engineering to government agencies. Its public-interest work spans national security, cybersecurity, healthcare, transportation, and government modernization. [group-profile](https://www.mitre.org/who-we-are)
