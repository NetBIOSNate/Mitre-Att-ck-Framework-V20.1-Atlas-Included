# AML.E0008 - Tenable

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Tenable](AML.E0008%20-%20Tenable.md) is a cybersecurity company providing exposure-management technology to help organizations identify, prioritize, and remediate security risks across their attack surfaces. Their research organization, Tenable Research, investigates vulnerabilities, emerging threats, and technologies including AI, and publishes security advisories and research findings.
