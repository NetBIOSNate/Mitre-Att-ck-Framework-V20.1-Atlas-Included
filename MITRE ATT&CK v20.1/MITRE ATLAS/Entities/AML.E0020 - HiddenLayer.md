# AML.E0020 - HiddenLayer

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[HiddenLayer](AML.E0020%20-%20HiddenLayer.md) is an AI security company founded by cybersecurity researchers. Their research examines adversarial techniques affecting predictive, generative, and agentic AI and informs their security products and services. They publish vulnerability findings, frameworks, and defensive methodologies and collaborate with the broader security community. [group-profile](https://www.hiddenlayer.com/about-us)

**Entity type**  Organization
