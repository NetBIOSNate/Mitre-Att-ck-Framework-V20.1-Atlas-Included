# AML.E0016 - Cato Networks

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Cato Networks](AML.E0016%20-%20Cato%20Networks.md) provides a cloud-based secure access service edge (SASE) platform combining networking and security. In September 2025, they acquired Aim Security to expand their capabilities for securing enterprise AI adoption. [aim-acquisition](https://www.catonetworks.com/news/cato-acquires-aim-security-to-extend-sase-leadership-and-secure-enterprise-ai-transformation/) Their research organizations include Cato CTRL, which investigates cyber threats, and Cato AI Labs, formerly the research department of Aim Security. [group-profile](https://www.catonetworks.com/cato-ctrl/) [ai-labs-profile](https://www.catonetworks.com/blog/author/itay-ravia/)

**Entity type**  Organization

## Associated entities

- Cato CTRL — The threat-intelligence and security-research team at Cato Networks. [group-profile](https://www.catonetworks.com/cato-ctrl/)
