# AML.E0040 - Alibaba Group

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Entities](README.md)

[Alibaba Group](AML.E0040%20-%20Alibaba%20Group.md) is a Chinese technology company founded in 1999, with businesses spanning commerce, cloud computing, and AI. They develop the Qwen family of language and multimodal models and provide AI applications, enterprise services, and computing infrastructure. Their commerce platforms provide technology and services that help merchants, brands, retailers, and small businesses reach customers and operate online.
