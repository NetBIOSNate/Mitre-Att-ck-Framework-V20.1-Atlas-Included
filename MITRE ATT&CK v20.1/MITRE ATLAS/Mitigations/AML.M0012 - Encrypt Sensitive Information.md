# AML.M0012 - Encrypt Sensitive Information

[MITRE ATLAS](../README.md) / [Mitigations](README.md)

Encrypt sensitive data such as AI models to protect against adversaries attempting to access sensitive data.

**ATT&CK mapping**  [Local ATT&CK M1041](../../Enterprise/Defenses/Mitigations/M1041%20-%20Encrypt%20Sensitive%20Information.md) · [MITRE ATT&CK M1041](https://attack.mitre.org/mitigations/M1041/)

**Mitigation categories**  Technical - Cyber

**Lifecycle phases**  Data Preparation, AI Model Engineering, Deployment

- [AML.T0007 - Discover AI Artifacts](../Tactics/11%20AML.TA0008%20-%20Discovery/AML.T0007%20-%20Discover%20AI%20Artifacts/README.md) — Encrypting AI artifacts can protect against adversary attempts to discover sensitive information.
- [AML.T0035 - AI Artifact Collection](../Tactics/13%20AML.TA0009%20-%20Collection/AML.T0035%20-%20AI%20Artifact%20Collection/README.md) — Protect machine learning artifacts with encryption.

- [AML.T0063 - Discover AI Model Outputs](../Tactics/11%20AML.TA0008%20-%20Discovery/AML.T0063%20-%20Discover%20AI%20Model%20Outputs/README.md) — Encrypting model outputs can prevent adversaries from discovering sensitive information about the AI-enabled system or its operations.
