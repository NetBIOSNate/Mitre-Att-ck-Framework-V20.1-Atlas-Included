# AML.S0018 - Xanthorox

[MITRE ATLAS](../../README.md) / [Software](../README.md)

[Xanthorox](AML.S0018%20-%20Xanthorox.md) is an underground AI service marketed for generating malware, ransomware, and phishing content. It integrates third-party AI services, including Gemini, with open-source components such as Crush, Hexstrike AI, LibreChat-AI, and Open WebUI through Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers. Its advertised claims of a bespoke, self-hosted model conflict with the commercial-model dependencies identified in its implementation. [source-1](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/distillation-experimentation-integration-ai-adversarial-use)
