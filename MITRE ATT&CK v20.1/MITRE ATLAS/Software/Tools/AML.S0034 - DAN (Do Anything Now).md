# AML.S0034 - DAN (Do Anything Now)

[MITRE ATLAS](../../README.md) / [Software](../README.md)

[DAN (Do Anything Now)](AML.S0034%20-%20DAN%20%28Do%20Anything%20Now%29.md) is a family of reusable jailbreak prompts that instruct a language model to roleplay an unrestricted assistant. Published variants define an alternative persona, direct the model to disregard its normal restrictions, and may request separate standard and unrestricted answers. Some variants introduce fictional token rewards or penalties and reminder phrases to reinforce the persona across subsequent exchanges. [canonical-source](https://github.com/0xk1h0/ChatGPT_DAN)

**Software type**  Tool

**Aliases**  DAN

## Techniques implemented

- [AML.T0054 - LLM Jailbreak](../../Tactics/08%20AML.TA0012%20-%20Privilege%20Escalation/AML.T0054%20-%20LLM%20Jailbreak/README.md) — [DAN (Do Anything Now)](AML.S0034%20-%20DAN%20%28Do%20Anything%20Now%29.md) attempts to bypass model safeguards by instructing the model to adopt a persona that must answer requests without refusals or content restrictions. Variants reinforce these instructions through paired standard and unrestricted responses, fictional penalties for refusing, and follow-up reminders to remain in character.
