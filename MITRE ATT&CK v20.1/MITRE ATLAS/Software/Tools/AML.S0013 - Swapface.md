# AML.S0013 - Swapface

[MITRE ATLAS](../../README.md) / [Software](../README.md)

[Swapface](AML.S0013%20-%20Swapface.md) is an AI face-swapping application for video calls and live streaming. It replaces a face in an incoming video feed with a selected or uploaded facial image and sends the resulting video to compatible applications through virtual camera outputs, including SwapFaceCam and OBS Virtual Camera. [source-1](https://swapface.org/)
